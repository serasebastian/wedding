Notre histoire

Our love story

  1. 1 June 2023

    The first date

    It all started in Amsterdam. Sera was late and at the wrong place. Not exactly a flawless beginning, but apparently a good one. Two days later, we met again at the Young Patrons Gala. From that evening on, we were rarely apart.

  2. August 2023

    Champagne

    Our first trip together took us to the Champagne region. We always knew we would return.

  3. 1 September 2023

    It’s official

    After three long months, Sebas finally made it official and asked Sera to be his girlfriend.

  4. October 2024

    Moving in

    A year later, we moved in together at Sebas’ apartment on Warmondstraat in Amsterdam.

  5. June 2025

    Our own place

    We bought our first apartment together and found a place to truly call our own.

  6. 1 August 2026

    A thousand times yes

    On top of Mount Rigi in Switzerland, Sebas asked Sera to marry him. The answer was an easy one.

  7. 1 May 2027

    The civil wedding

    On 1 May 2027, we will officially tie the knot at our civil wedding. As you may have noticed, we seem to have a thing for the first of the month when it comes to special moments.

  8. We say “I do”

    And now, back where one of our first adventures began, we can’t wait to celebrate the next chapter with all of you.

Bon à savoir

Good to know

Can I bring my children or a guest?

This is an intimate gathering, so we kindly ask that only the guests named on your invitation attend. While we adore your little ones, the weekend is for adults only. Thank you for understanding.

Where should I stay?

We recommend ARBELLIA Appart’Hôtel in Châlons-en-Champagne, about twenty minutes from the château, and the LOISIUM Wine & Spa Hotel at Mutigny, in the vineyards above Aÿ. Both are under Où dormir.

Please book as early as you can. We are not the only ones getting married in the region that weekend, and rooms nearby fill up quickly. Beyond the hotels there are also many lovely chambres d’hôtes around the villages, which are often the nicest way to stay.

Where can I park?

There is parking at the château itself, on the right-hand side of the estate and a short walk from the courtyard. You’ll find it as number 6 on the plan of the grounds. If you need accessible parking or other assistance, please let the maîtres de cérémonie know.

When should I reply?

Please reply by 28 February 2027 using the RSVP form. You can change your reply at any time before then by simply submitting the form again.
